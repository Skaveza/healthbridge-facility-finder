What HealthBridge Does

Location-Based

Share your phone's location or type your subcounty, and see the nearest facilities with their distance from you.

Search by Service

Narrow the results to what you need, such as maternity care, a laboratory, a pharmacy, X-ray or immunisation, and to government or private facilities.

Contact Details

Phone numbers, opening hours and ratings are shown where the public data has them. Many facilities have gaps, so call before you travel.

Open About Its Limits

The data comes from public sources and is incomplete. We leave out facilities whose recorded location looks unreliable, so some real facilities will not appear.
