Location checks: a point is a placeholder if five or more facilities share exactly the same coordinates. A point contradicts its label if it is more than 50 km from the middle of its own subcounty, checked only for subcounties with at least three facilities. The source is a public Uganda healthcare facilities dataset from Pollicy.
